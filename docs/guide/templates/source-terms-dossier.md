# Source Terms Dossier

Use this template during [documentation and sampling review](../source-onboarding.md#1-document-terms).

## Source and documentation

- Qualified catalogue ID, source owner, investigator, and date.
- Official documentation, terms, licence, and native schema or codebook citations with versions
  and access dates.
- Supported methods, endpoint and host scope, redirect policy, and publication cadence.

## Rights and controls

- Permission for automation, storage and sampling, with the relevant terms citations.
- Retention, redistribution and export rights, including attribution and field restrictions.
- Published rate limits, conditional request support, access restrictions, and approved credential
  alias if needed.
- Unresolved rights or access questions and disposition (`candidate` or `rejected` with reason).

## Sampling decision

- Proposed data subset, time window, method, content types, and allowed hosts and redirects.
- Request count, deadline, response and decoded byte limits, and rate budget.
- Archive location and limits on retention, access and use.
- Owner decision (approved, narrowed or rejected), approver, timestamp, decision reference,
  expiry, and conditions. Pending decisions stay with the human-assisted task.
