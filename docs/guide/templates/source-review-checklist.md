# Source Review Checklist

Use this checklist for [review and merge](../source-onboarding.md#5-review-and-merge)
and for [enablement](../source-onboarding.md#6-obtain-enablement-approval).

- [ ] Record the qualified source ID, reviewed revision, reviewer, and UTC review date.
- [ ] Confirm the dossier, rights for the reviewed use, sampling authorization and budget.
- [ ] Reconcile the capture and actual budget against the investigation report.
- [ ] Inspect sanitized fixtures and their permitted use. Confirm the fixture check passed.
- [ ] Review native keys, versions, mapping and quality rules, parser, and error handling.
- [ ] Verify access, retention, redistribution and export limits in the crawler configuration.
- [ ] Record `kn source validate`, fixture test results, JUnit evidence, and `make ci`.
- [ ] Record earlier source suites and fixture-archive rebuild evidence where applicable.
- [ ] Record the review outcome and merged revision.
- [ ] Record the separate owner enablement decision, scope, approver, date and reference.
- [ ] Validate the approved enabled configuration before scheduling and record the result.
